## What lines and circles add

1. Start with the points 0 and 1. Where two lines cross, you only get numbers you could already reach by adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing.
2. Where a circle crosses a line, you solve a quadratic. So each circle can add one square root, and nothing else.

## Fields, and degree

1. A world where you can add, subtract, multiply, and divide by anything but zero is a field. The rationals are one. So is the seven-hour clock.
2. Add root 2 to the rationals and you get every a plus b root 2: a grid with two directions. Its degree is 2.

## The tower law

1. Add root 3 on top, and each direction splits in two again: 1, root 2, root 3, root 6. Degrees multiply: 2 times 2 is 4.
2. Each compass step adds a floor that at most doubles the degree. So anything you can construct has degree 1, 2, 4, 8: always a power of 2.

## Payoff: cos 20° has degree 3

1. Now the angle. Cutting 60 degrees in three means building the cosine of 20 degrees, a root of 8x cubed minus 6x minus 1.
2. That cubic won't factor, so the cosine of 20 degrees has degree 3. Three isn't a power of 2. No construction can ever reach it.
3. The same 3 rules out doubling the cube. Two thousand years of attempts, closed by counting dimensions.
