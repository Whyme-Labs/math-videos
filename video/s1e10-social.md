**Thread** (4 posts):

1. `The square root of −1 is a rotation, and you can see it. 🧵`
2. `Build ℂ as pairs of reals. Add piece by piece. Multiply with one odd rule: (a, b)(c, d) = (ac − bd, ad + bc). Call (0, 1) "i". Then i · i = (−1, 0).`
3. `That odd rule is rotation. Every complex number has a length and an angle, and multiplying by it stretches by the length and turns by the angle. (3 + 4i)(1 + 2i): lengths 5 and √5 multiply, angles 53° and 63° add.`
4. `The payoff: every polynomial has a root in ℂ. The price: ℂ can't be ordered, because in an ordered field squares are positive, and i² = −1. Episode 10: <YouTube link>`
