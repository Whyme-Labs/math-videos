## Cold open: the traveler at midnight

1. It's midnight, and it's raining. A traveler with one suitcase reaches the only hotel in town.
2. The hotel has infinitely many rooms, numbered 0, 1, 2, and on forever. Tonight every room is taken.
3. The clerk picks up the intercom: everyone, please move from your room to the next room along.
4. Every guest steps one door along. Nobody leaves, nobody shares, and room 0 is empty. The traveler gets the key.
5. The hotel was full, and now it has room. So what does full even mean, when you can't count to the end?

## An infinite bus

1. At one in the morning a bus pulls up with infinitely many passengers: seat 0, seat 1, seat 2, and on.
2. The clerk calls: everyone in room n, move to room 2n. Every guest lands on an even door, and every odd door is free.
3. Seat k takes room 2k plus 1. An infinite busload, seated, and nobody had to leave.

## Same size means matchable

1. So are there fewer even numbers than whole numbers? The evens look like only half of them.
2. Pair them up instead: 0 with 0, 1 with 2, 2 with 4. Every number gets exactly one partner, and nobody is left over.
3. Galileo saw this in 1638 with square numbers, and gave up comparing infinities. Cantor kept the pairing instead.
4. Two sets have the same size if they can be matched one to one. That is the whole definition. No counting needed.

## Infinitely many buses

1. At two in the morning, infinitely many buses arrive, and every bus has infinitely many passengers.
2. Seat them bus by bus and it fails: bus 1 alone takes every free room, and bus 2 waits forever.
3. Instead, walk the car park in diagonals. Each diagonal is short, so every passenger on every bus gets a turn.

## The fractions grow on a tree

1. The fractions feel even bigger. Between any two of them there are infinitely many more.
2. But they grow on a tree. Start at 1 over 1; each a over b has two children, a over a plus b, and a plus b over b.
3. Every positive fraction grows on it exactly once, in lowest terms. Read it row by row, and each one gets a room.
