1. A square has 8 symmetries. Each one moves the plane.
2. A quarter turn: r = [0 −1; 1 0].

5. A representation turns each g into a matrix ρ(g).

## Schur's lemma

1. Schur: take a map that commutes with every ρ(g).
2. Its kernel and its image are invariant subspaces.
3. Between irreducibles: zero, or an isomorphism.
4. From an irreducible to itself: only a scalar, λI.
