# Posting on each platform

How to write titles, descriptions and captions for each platform, and what
format each one wants. Hard limits change often and third-party sources
disagree. The numbers here are working limits. Check the uploader before
relying on one. Last checked October 2026.

## Common rules

- **Lead with the cold-open question**, not the series name. "Does 0.999…
  equal 1?" gets people to tap. "S1E01" does not. Put the series and episode
  number at the end of the title or in the description.
- **Promise only what the video delivers.** S1E01 asks the three questions
  and says which episodes answer them. A title like "0.999… = 1, proven"
  would be clickbait. "Does 0.999… equal 1? Then what is a number?" is
  honest.
- **One language per post.** English posts go to YouTube, X, LinkedIn and
  TikTok. Chinese posts go to bilibili and 抖音. YouTube also gets a separate
  Chinese upload, or the Chinese title and description added as a
  translation.
- **The voice rules apply**, along with the unslop and humanizer passes. No
  "🤯", no "you won't believe", no "mind-blowing". At most one or two emoji
  per post, and only where a platform's culture expects them (TikTok,
  抖音). Hashtags go at the end.
- **Credit the music** when the platform has a field for it, and don't add
  platform music on top. The background track is licensed separately.
  Confirm the licence covers the platform before posting.

## Format per platform

| Platform | Language | Best format | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube | EN (+ ZH upload) | full 16:9 episode, chapters, Shorts cut of the cold open | home of the series |
| bilibili | ZH | full 16:9 episode, 分段章节, 封面 | 知识区 audience watches long-form |
| X | EN | 30–60 s clip + link, or full video with Premium | free accounts: about 140 s max |
| LinkedIn | EN | full video natively or a clip, posted as Whyme Labs | builder or educator angle, not a hook-y caption |
| TikTok | EN | 9:16 vertical cut, 30–90 s | horizontal full episodes do poorly |
| 抖音 | ZH | 9:16 vertical cut, 30–90 s | same cut as TikTok, Chinese edition |

A vertical cut means re-laying out a scene for 1080×1920, not cropping the
16:9 frame. The cold open (three questions) and one payoff scene make the
best shorts. Build it as its own composition when needed.

## YouTube

- **Title:** at most 100 characters, and the first ~60 show in search.
  Pattern: `<question>? | What Is a Number? S1E01`.
- **Description:** the first ~150 characters show above "more", so put the
  hook and the promise there. Then the chapters, then the series and
  links, then credits.
- **Chapters:** the first is `0:00`, there are at least 3, and each is at
  least 10 s long. Take them from the scene starts (crossfade midpoints).
  Merge the brand sting and title into the first chapter.
- **Hashtags:** 3 in the description. The first three show above the title.
- **Tags field:** the topic words (number theory, Peano axioms, set theory,
  0.999 = 1, square root of 2, infinity).

## X (x.com)

- 280 characters for standard accounts. A link counts as 23.
- One post with the hook and a clip, then the YouTube link in a reply or at
  the end. A short thread (3–4 posts, one question each) works well for the
  three questions.
- Hashtags are optional. Use at most 1–2 (#math, #maths).

## LinkedIn

- **Worth posting**, as the Whyme Labs company page (reshared by the
  founder). The audience there responds to "we're building this" and "here's
  how we made it": the math, plus the craft (Remotion, beat-synced edit,
  bilingual from one codebase).
- Up to 3,000 characters. The first ~210 show before "see more".
- Write in the first person, in short paragraphs. End with a real question
  for the reader, not "Thoughts?". Use 3–5 hashtags.

## TikTok

- The caption can be long, but only the first line or two show over the
  video. Write one hook line, one line of context, and 3–5 hashtags.
- Put on-screen text in the first second. The cold open's questions are
  perfect for it.
- Use the "Part 1" pattern when posting a series of shorts.

## bilibili

- **标题** at most 80 characters. B站 viewers expect a fuller title, often
  with 【】 for the series: `【数是什么？S1E01】0.999…等于1吗？根号2是数吗？`
- **简介:** a hook, chapters, the series plan and references. Credit the
  music and fonts.
- **标签:** up to 10. Use 数学, 科普, 知识, 数学史, 皮亚诺公理, 集合论 and so on.
- **分区:** 知识 → 科学科普. Mark it 自制.
- **分段章节:** add chapters with the same timestamps as the description.
- **封面:** 16:10 (1146×717 works). Use the title card's look with a question
  in big type.

## 抖音

- Short title and caption. Lead with the question and keep it under about
  30 characters on one line so it doesn't get cut. Add 3–5 话题 like
  #数学 #科普 #涨知识.
- Vertical 9:16 cut, ideally under 90 s. Link to the full episode on
  bilibili in the comments or the 合集 description, if the account allows
  it.
- Use the 合集 feature for the series.
